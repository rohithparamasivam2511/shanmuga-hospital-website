எங்களை பற்றி | About Kavin Medical Center

Kavin Medical Center is an advanced multispecialty and super-specialty healthcare institution situated strategically at Gandhi Nagar on the Erode–Perundurai Main Road, Erode.

Led by renowned medical director Dr. G. I. Nambi (M.S., M.Ch.), our hospital stands as a center of surgical excellence across Western Tamil Nadu. We specialize in intricate plastic & reconstructive surgery, dedicated acute burn rehabilitation, hand trauma surgery, laparoscopic operations, diabetic foot salvage, and comprehensive round-the-clock emergency medical services.

  • Pioneer Center for Plastic, Microvascular & Burn Surgery
  • Ultra-Modern Modular OTs, High-Tech ICU & Critical Care
  • Dedicated Diabetic Foot Ulcer Salvage & Vascular Clinic
  • Compassionate bilingual patient care (Tamil & English)
