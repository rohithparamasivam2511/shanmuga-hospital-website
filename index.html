Shanmuga hospital - MECHERI has been serving the people of Mecheri and surrounding villages since 2004. Located in the heart of the community, we are the most accessible and trusted hospital in this region.

We combine modern medical technology with compassionate, community-focused care to ensure the best outcomes for every patient — from a simple consultation to complex surgery.

  • Licensed by Tamil Nadu Health Department
  • Modern OT, ICU & Diagnostic Labs
  • 24/7 Emergency & Ambulance Services
  • Bilingual staff (Tamil & English)
